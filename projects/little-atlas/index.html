WELCOME ABOARD

Your first flight

  1. Pick a name from the tray to carry it.
  2. Fly to its state. Drag the joystick, drag the globe, or use WASD / arrow keys. The ring beneath your plane is the exact target.
  3. Place the name with the button or Space. You can also drag a name directly onto the map.

Zoom in for small states. Pinch, use + / −, or scroll over the globe. US brings you home; ◎ shows the whole world.

Explore reveals the state beneath you and pauses placing. Return to the game when you're ready.

About this little world

U.S. boundaries: us-atlas 3.0.1, derived from the U.S. Census Bureau. World land: world-atlas 2.0.2, derived from Natural Earth. Simplified boundaries are intended for a game, not navigation.

Rendered with D3 7.9.0 and TopoJSON Client 3.1.0. Their licenses are bundled with this app. The airplane is an original SVG drawing.

Only the fifty U.S. states are scored in this expedition. The rest of the globe is open for flying.
